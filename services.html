Security solutions

Our Services

Professional, tailored protection for venues, sites, businesses and public events.

Licensed premises

Door Supervision

We provide trained, SIA-licensed door supervisors for nightclubs, pubs, bars and licensed entertainment venues. Our staff support both security and the customer experience.

  • Checking identification and supporting venue admission policies
  • Managing queues and maintaining safe entry and exit routes
  • Preventing and managing conflict through calm communication
  • Refusing entry appropriately where venue conditions are not met
  • Monitoring capacity, behaviour and potential safeguarding concerns
  • Liaising with management, emergency services and local police when required
  • Recording incidents accurately and preserving relevant evidence
Sites and premises

Security Guarding

Our guarding services provide a visible, professional security presence to protect people, property, equipment and business operations.

  • Gatehouse control and verification of authorised visitors
  • Construction-site security and protection of plant and materials
  • Office, corporate building and business-park security
  • Retail and shopping-centre guarding
  • Routine patrols, lock and unlock duties and alarm response support
  • Contractor, delivery and vehicle access management
  • Incident escalation, daily occurrence books and client reporting
Crowd safety

Event Management

We help organisers manage crowds safely at football matches, concerts, festivals, community events and other public gatherings.

  • Entry-point and ticket-checking support
  • Queue, crowd-flow and restricted-area management
  • Front-of-stage, perimeter and backstage security
  • Monitoring for hazards, disorder and safeguarding concerns
  • Supporting emergency, evacuation and lost-person procedures
  • Professional liaison with event management and emergency services
  • Post-event dispersal and incident reporting
Monitoring

CCTV Monitoring

Our CCTV personnel support active surveillance, early incident identification and coordinated response. Services can be integrated with guarding and event operations.

  • Monitoring camera systems in accordance with site procedures
  • Identifying suspicious activity, safety risks and access breaches
  • Maintaining accurate incident and observation records
  • Coordinating with mobile, guarding or management teams
  • Handling footage and personal data in line with applicable procedures

Professional security services delivered by trained, experienced and SIA-licensed personnel across the United Kingdom.
