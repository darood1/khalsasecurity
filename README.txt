KHalsa Security Website
========================

Open index.html in a browser.

Pages:
- index.html
- services.html
- vacancies.html
- contact.html

Folders:
- assets/css/styles.css
- assets/js/script.js
- assets/images/

Forms:
The contact and vacancy forms open the visitor's email program using mailto.
For server-side delivery, connect the forms to your hosting provider or a form service.

Replace or add images in assets/images/ and update the corresponding image paths in the HTML.
